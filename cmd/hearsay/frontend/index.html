Install the Hearsay app on your phone

Hearsay needs an Android phone with Gboard on the same Wi-Fi as this computer. Skip this if the app is already installed.

  1. Turn Play Protect off for a moment: in the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect, then the gear, and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. It blocks apps it doesn't know.
  2. Scan the code with the phone's camera and download the app.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  4. Turn Play Protect back on.

Or open on the phone. Have a USB cable and Developer options? hearsay setup --adb in a terminal does most of this for you.

Turn on the key presser

The app taps the keyboard's mic key for you. Android calls this an accessibility service.

  1. Open the Hearsay app and tap Open settings next to Key presser, then turn on Hearsay key presser.
  2. If Android says it's a restricted setting: go back to the app, tap App info, then the ⋮ menu, Allow restricted settings, and try again.
  3. On Android 17, allow the app on the local network when it asks.
